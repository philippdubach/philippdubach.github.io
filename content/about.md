He uses large-scale datasets, market microstructure analysis, causal methods, and machine learning to study financial markets and digital behavior. His research covers prediction-market order books, options and volatility, asset allocation, synthetic consumer populations, online attention, and consumer protection in algorithmic markets.

Papers are available on [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/cf_dev/AbsByAuth.cfm?per_id=9538192#form1) and [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/search/q-fin?searchtype=author&query=Dubach,+P+D) and indexed by [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=-69oS2wAAAAJ) and [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Philipp-Dubach); the full publication record is on [Research](/research/). Essays and open-source [projects](/projects/) are read by portfolio managers, CTOs, founders, and researchers.
