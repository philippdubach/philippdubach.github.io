## Phase 1 -- SERP Analysis

### 1. enterprise AI revenue 2025

- **Format dominance:** Market reports, adoption surveys, funding profiles, and vendor-by-vendor summaries.
- **Site types ranking:** Venture firms, research organisations, publishers, consultancies, company databases, and PDFs.
- **Content freshness:** Mostly late 2025 and 2026, with fast turnover as private-company run rates change.
- **Common headings/sections:** Enterprise adoption, application versus infrastructure spend, vendor share, industry use cases, and growth rates.
- **Gaps:** Results often equate buyer spending, annual recurring revenue, run rate, and recognized revenue. Few show structural double-counts.
- **SERP features:** Reports, PDFs, news results, and company/reference panels. The sampled results did not show a stable PAA box.

### 2. AI capex revenue gap

- **Format dominance:** Investment commentary, bubble analysis, explainers, charts, and discussion threads.
- **Site types ranking:** Financial publishers, investment firms, independent analysts, research papers, and communities.
- **Content freshness:** High; most results are 2025-2026 and update after earnings guidance.
- **Common headings/sections:** Revenue required, capex forecasts, depreciation, free cash flow, funding gaps, and bubble comparisons.
- **Gaps:** Many pages rely on a single top-down revenue estimate. This post distinguishes four legitimate perimeters and supplies an audit-grade floor.
- **SERP features:** News, discussions, PDF research, and image/chart-led results.

### 3. hyperscaler AI capex 2026

- **Format dominance:** Trackers, company tables, credit notes, news analysis, and supply-chain maps.
- **Site types ranking:** Credit agencies, banks, data-centre publishers, market trackers, investment managers, and specialist analysts.
- **Content freshness:** Extremely high because company guidance changes quarterly.
- **Common headings/sections:** Spending by company, AI share of total capex, data-centre assets, depreciation, debt, backlog, and historical comparisons.
- **Gaps:** Most trackers stop at the spending side. The post supplies an enterprise-revenue crosswalk and capex-coverage ratios.
- **SERP features:** Fresh news, tables, PDFs, and tracker pages.

### 4. Gartner AI spending 2025

- **Format dominance:** Gartner releases, syndicated news, forecast summaries, and commentary.
- **Site types ranking:** Gartner, business publishers, consultancies, and video/news outlets.
- **Content freshness:** Concentrated around Gartner's 2025 forecast releases, with later 2026 retrospectives.
- **Common headings/sections:** Total spending, devices, servers, software, services, growth, and CIO adoption.
- **Gaps:** Summaries repeat the headline without separating full-retail device value from vendor-recognized enterprise AI revenue.
- **SERP features:** Featured numerical snippets, news, video, and tables.

### 5. Menlo enterprise AI 2025

- **Format dominance:** First-party report, PDF, press release, news summaries, and video commentary.
- **Site types ranking:** Menlo Ventures dominates, followed by syndication services, publishers, and community discussions.
- **Content freshness:** Concentrated in December 2025, with 2026 pages citing the report.
- **Common headings/sections:** $37 billion spend, survey method, application share, infrastructure share, model/vendor share, and adoption.
- **Gaps:** Most summaries do not stress the US-only, generative-only, enterprise-only perimeter or reconcile it with global estimates.
- **SERP features:** PDF, video, images/charts, and press-release results.
