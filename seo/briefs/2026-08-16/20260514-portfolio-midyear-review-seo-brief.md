## Phase 1 -- SERP Analysis

Research combined four discovery searches with one focused search for each selected keyword. Findings are qualitative; no search-volume figures or authority scores were invented.

### 1. midyear portfolio review 2026

- **Format dominance**: Personal portfolio updates, institutional midyear outlooks, model-portfolio reviews, and ETF allocation commentaries.
- **Site types ranking**: This article, global asset managers, private banks, investment publishers, and model-portfolio providers.
- **Content freshness**: Extremely high because the query is year-specific. Most relevant results were published between May and July 2026.
- **Common headings/sections**: Year-to-date return, benchmarks, winners and losers, macro backdrop, valuations, allocation changes, and second-half outlook.
- **Gaps**: Institutional outlooks rarely publish a falsifiable prior thesis or admit specific errors. The article compares each December call with realised CHF outcomes.
- **SERP features**: Dated results, current-outlook modules, charts, and result snippets with performance figures.

### 2. portfolio performance review

- **Format dominance**: Portfolio-tracking software reviews, templates, checklists, and advisory-process pages.
- **Site types ranking**: Affiliate/review sites, analytics tools, adviser resources, and template publishers.
- **Content freshness**: High for software pages; evergreen for review-process guides.
- **Common headings/sections**: Time-weighted return, benchmark, cash flows, risk, concentration, attribution, actions, and review cadence.
- **Gaps**: Many results describe a process but do not show an honest worked example with mistakes, currency effects, and invalidation rules.
- **SERP features**: Product-review snippets, checklist formatting, FAQs, and comparison content. The query is ambiguous, so “investment portfolio” context matters.

### 3. Shiller CAPE ratio 2026

- **Format dominance**: Data pages, valuation commentary, market outlooks, news analysis, videos, and investor discussion.
- **Site types ranking**: Market-data sites, asset managers, financial publishers, reference sites, and investor forums.
- **Content freshness**: Very high because the ratio changes with price and earnings data.
- **Common headings/sections**: Definition, current level, long-run mean, dot-com comparison, expected returns, methodological limits, and market timing.
- **Gaps**: Alarmist pages treat CAPE as a crash clock. The article treats it as a valuation constraint while acknowledging earnings support and persistent expensive markets.
- **SERP features**: Current-number snippets, charts, news discussions, and videos.

### 4. currency hedging Swiss investors

- **Format dominance**: Academic papers, wealth-manager explainers, institutional guidance, bank publications, and investor discussions.
- **Site types ranking**: University of Basel/RePEc, Swiss investment consultants, asset managers, banks, and academic journals.
- **Content freshness**: Strong. Results include 2025 guidance and a 2026 Swiss study alongside foundational research.
- **Common headings/sections**: CHF safe-haven behaviour, equity versus bond hedging, interest-rate differentials, forward contracts, carry cost, and strategic hedge ratios.
- **Gaps**: General pages often prescribe one policy. The post demonstrates asset- and currency-specific decisions: hedge low-cost yen exposure but leave costly dollar exposure open.
- **SERP features**: PDF papers, direct-answer headings, tables, and numerical carry examples.

### 5. portfolio rebalancing 2026

- **Format dominance**: Guides, institutional education, tools, current market commentaries, and videos.
- **Site types ranking**: Vanguard, financial publishers, wealth platforms, model-portfolio providers, and adviser sites.
- **Content freshness**: High because many pages were refreshed for 2026, although the principles are evergreen.
- **Common headings/sections**: Why rebalance, calendar versus threshold rules, allocation drift, taxes, transaction costs, risk tolerance, and using new cash.
- **Gaps**: Generic guides do not show how a thesis review can lead to small changes rather than wholesale reversal. The post uses new cash and natural drift to limit trading.
- **SERP features**: How-to snippets, checklists, videos, and frequently asked questions.

## Phase 5 -- Content Differentiation

The post publishes a genuine prior and evaluates it in the author's base currency. It separates correct outcomes from incorrect causal stories—for example, emerging markets worked because of Korean and Taiwanese chipmakers, not expected Chinese stimulus.

It also combines return attribution with intellectual accountability. The article names the clean rate miss, identifies currency effects, measures hidden AI exposure, and states what would change each remaining thesis.

The use of a time-weighted CHF return, a global 60/40 benchmark, detailed charts, and small implementation choices creates stronger experience and trust signals than generic outlook pages.
