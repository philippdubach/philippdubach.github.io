## Phase 1 -- SERP Analysis

### 1. Leopold Aschenbrenner predictions

- **Format dominance:** Profiles, prediction trackers, summaries, retrospective commentary, and discussions.
- **Site types ranking:** Publishers, reference sites, specialist AI sites, forums, and the original essay.
- **Content freshness:** Mixed. The original 2024 coverage remains visible, while 2026 trackers and fund reporting add retrospectives.
- **Common headings/sections:** AGI timeline, automated AI research, superintelligence, compute, power, China, security, and the investment fund.
- **Gaps:** Most pages list claims without grading their evidence, deadlines, causal substrate, or political-versus-technical split.
- **SERP features:** News, PDFs, discussion results, reference panels, and prediction trackers.

### 2. Situational Awareness AGI 2027

- **Format dominance:** Essay summaries, explainers, debates, forecasts, and the source PDF.
- **Site types ranking:** The original site, publishers, LessWrong-style communities, academic commentary, and reference sites.
- **Content freshness:** Foundational 2024 results remain strong; 2025-2026 discussions ask whether the window is still open.
- **Common headings/sections:** OOM counting, unhobbling, drop-in remote workers, automated AI research, and intelligence explosion.
- **Gaps:** Summary pages rarely separate pretraining scale from test-time compute and RL post-training evidence.
- **SERP features:** PDF results, snippets with the 2027 quote, discussions, and related AGI forecasts.

### 3. Situational Awareness essay summary

- **Format dominance:** Condensed summaries, multi-part guides, videos, and reading notes.
- **Site types ranking:** Independent writers, newsletters, LessWrong, reference pages, and content aggregators.
- **Content freshness:** Mostly 2024, with new summaries still appearing in 2026.
- **Common headings/sections:** OOMs, AGI, superintelligence, security, alignment, geopolitics, and the industrial buildout.
- **Gaps:** Summaries compress the argument but rarely test it against subsequent events or disclose the investment implications.
- **SERP features:** Long snippets, PDF/source links, video, and translated summaries.

### 4. Leopold Aschenbrenner Manhattan Project

- **Format dominance:** Essay excerpts, commentary, debates, historical analogies, and policy papers.
- **Site types ranking:** The original essay, publishers, forums, academic work, and policy commentary.
- **Content freshness:** The 2024 proposal remains the anchor; later results compare it with Stargate, defence contracting, and AGI planning.
- **Common headings/sections:** Nationalisation, lab security, SCIFs, US-China competition, allied coordination, and nuclear analogies.
- **Gaps:** Most results argue for or against the project. This post tests whether the analogy has the physical and institutional substrate it requires.
- **SERP features:** PDF, discussion results, academic papers, and related Manhattan Project queries.

### 5. Situational Awareness LP portfolio

- **Format dominance:** 13F databases, portfolio trackers, investor profiles, holding tables, and fund-thesis analysis.
- **Site types ranking:** Filing aggregators, portfolio tools, financial publishers, independent research, and community discussion.
- **Content freshness:** Very high and quarterly, because each new 13F changes the visible holdings.
- **Common headings/sections:** Top holdings, position changes, power and compute themes, option exposure, concentration, and performance.
- **Gaps:** Trackers can show holdings but not shorts, private assets, trade timing, or the intellectual chain behind them. Many also confuse notional option exposure with capital at risk.
- **SERP features:** Tables, FAQ blocks, portfolio charts, and recent news.

## Phase 5 -- Content Differentiation

The post's strongest advantage is its dated, domain-by-domain audit. It gives credit for test-time compute, benchmark saturation, power, gas, and Gulf deal flow while separating those results from political prescriptions that remained open or moved backwards. The libertarian/Burkean-hawk/alignment-optimist “series circuit” and substrate-sensitivity framework are original contributions. The fund section adds a rare conflict-of-interest and capital-allocation lens. Preserve these ideas; do not turn the article into a generic summary or current portfolio tracker.

- **Target ranking position:** Page one is realistic for “Leopold Aschenbrenner predictions scorecard” and “Situational Awareness predictions two years later.” Summary and portfolio head terms are crowded by source pages and frequently updated trackers.
